Reliability w=0.1+0.9*(1-binary_entropy_bits(p))*exp(-abs(p-p_prime)/0.1). p_prime uses the same +2% reflectance gain on valid support pixels, clipped to documented [0,1], before normalization. This is a proxy, not calibrated correctness. Offline error diagnostics use confidence >=0.9 and absolute probability change <=0.05, split by reference class; these thresholds never determine an inference router. Invalid/padded support pixels remain unchanged under the gain.

Solve in float64 using one accumulation of cached sufficient statistics and Cholesky:
`(Phi_q.T W Phi_q + lambda I + gamma D.T M D) delta = Phi_q.T W (t - m_S,q)`;
`D=Phi_q-Phi_f`; per-chip mass M sums to one; reliability-weighted W is normalized to unit sum. Lambda regularizes all 17 coordinates including intercept. Ordinary FP has D=0. Common eta=min(1,1/max(RMS(Phi_q delta),1e-12)); record both undamped and applied delta. Undo standardization, add half update to water and subtract half from land. No additional deployed features/teacher calls. SGD uses identical cached pixels/features and teacher probabilities at inherited KD temperature4, plus the same source-selected lambda.
